# Proper-noun list for wordseg-rs.
#
# wordninja's word list is frequency-ordered corpus data, so product names and
# modern technical terms are missing from it entirely. An out-of-vocabulary
# token costs +inf, which forces the splitter to spell it out of ordinary words:
# "kubernetes" -> "ku berne tes". Every line here becomes a token the splitter
# may produce, and is restored to this exact spelling on the way out.
#
# One term per line. The lookup key is the line lowercased with spaces removed
# (so "Hugging Face" and "HuggingFace" collide, the last one winning), and the
# line itself is what gets written to stdout. Blank lines and lines starting
# with '#' are ignored.
#
# What belongs here, and what does not:
#
#   * Only terms that wordninja cannot split correctly on its own. Ordinary
#     words are deliberately excluded — a term that is also a common English
#     word ("fish", "bun", "kitty") would rewrite ordinary prose.
#   * Terms are priced as if they sat at rank 1_000_000 in the frequency list:
#     dearer than every real word, cheaper than the two or three words a failed
#     split spells them out of. A handful of terms that happen to decompose into
#     two very common words ("redis" = "red is", "niri" = "nir i") stay cheaper
#     to spell out, and are covered by the whole-run match instead.
#   * Keep entries to ASCII letters, digits and single spaces.
